# Tribute CSM Hub — CSM Onboarding Walkthrough

*Companion to the live session (rollout plan item L-2). Ten minutes, start to finish.*

## Getting in
Open the hub link in Chrome or Edge and sign in with your Tribute Microsoft account. You land directly in your own workspace — there is nothing to set up. Bookmark it.

## Your daily rhythm
1. **Home** — your stat tiles, high-priority tasks, to-dos, and *Needs Attention*. If you are covering for someone, a banner tells you.
2. **Client Pulse** — your caseload sorted by who needs you most. Red means act today; amber means keep an eye on it. Click a client to read the Pulse Read and the caregiver visit notes behind it.
3. **Clients** — the master table. Dots tell you at a glance whether visits, touchpoints and care conversations are on track. Click a row to expand: log a visit, touchpoint or care conversation, see Viv notes, or **Draft update** to have the assistant write a Tributey email you edit before sending.

## Things that happen for you
- **Viv sync** keeps clients, visits and open shifts current — you never enter a client by hand.
- **Calls and emails become touchpoints automatically** (marked ⟳). Inbound counts. You only log what the system can't see.
- **Success in 30** tracks new-client milestones; **Fill Priority** ranks open visits on your caseload.

## When you're out
Coverage Notes → **Handoffs**: pick who's covering, an end date, a note. Done. Your colleague sees a coverage profile per client. When you're back, *End my active handoffs*.

## When you're covering
Home banner → *Open coverage profiles*. Each card has the family contact, community numbers (from the **Communities** directory — add what you learn!), last visit, caregivers, the owner's notes, and the pulse.

## Questions the playbook answers
Tribute Assistant → **Ask the Tribute Assistant**. It answers from the SOPs and scenario guides and tells you when something isn't covered — then ask your Senior CSM.

## Something broken or missing?
Use the hub repo's Issues page (bug and idea templates) — or tell your Senior CSM. Every request is read.
