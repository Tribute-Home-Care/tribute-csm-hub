# Tribute CSM Hub — Market Director Demo (15 minutes)

*For the Chicago / Maryland / Massachusetts market director sessions (rollout plan item L-1).*

## The story in one line
The hub replaces scattered trackers with one place where every client's status is **known, current, and acted on** — and Tribute Intelligence tells the team *who needs attention today and why*.

## Demo flow

1. **Sign in → land in your space (1 min)**
   Microsoft sign-in, no folder picking, no passwords. CSMs land on their own caseload; managers see their market.

2. **Clients page (2 min)**
   Live from Viv every 30 minutes. Point out the cadence dots (visit / touchpoint / care conversation), the ⟳ Viv notes on a client card, and that auto-captured touchpoints (calls, emails) already count toward cadence — nobody has to log a phone call.

3. **Client Pulse (5 min) — the headline**
   Every client scored red / amber / green from caregiver visit notes, change-of-condition reports, service exceptions, and communications. Open the top red client: read the **Tribute Intelligence Pulse Read** aloud, then expand to show the caregiver notes it came from. *"That's a client we would have found out about two weeks later."*

4. **Manager Hub → Workload (2 min)**
   Workload = clients **plus** how much communication each client generates. Show two CSMs with similar client counts and very different bars. Coordinators are measured on market-wide support.

5. **Market Director view — Retention Risk (3 min)**
   Open the standalone Client Pulse page → *Market Director* toggle → your market. Preventable cancellation risks with drivers and a suggested intervention per client. *"This is your Monday morning list."* Then the Prediction vs Outcome tab: the tool grades itself.

6. **Coverage & Communities (1 min)**
   Hand a caseload to a covering CSM in two clicks; the covering CSM gets contacts, community numbers, notes and pulse in one card. Communities directory answers "who do I call at this building?"

7. **Close (1 min)**
   Time savings, consistency across markets, CSM independence — and the daily brief email when the team is ready for it.

## Talking points for questions
- **"Is the AI making decisions?"** It reads and flags; CSMs and managers decide. The rule-based signals stay visible on every card as the evidence trail.
- **"What about client privacy?"** Client data stays inside Tribute's Microsoft/Azure tenant; the AI read uses de-identified records.
- **"How current is it?"** Viv sync every 30 minutes on use; pulse rebuilds 3× a day plus each weekday at 7:30 AM.
